**Ember Court** — a turn-based card duel in the browser. You are Wren of the
Ember Court, facing Duchess Ashenvale across a felt table: 30 life each, a
30-card deck of your own, and four original suits — Flame, Tide, Thorn and
Gale — with sixteen cards of your own invention. Every turn you draw a card
and your ember grows (up to 10); spend it to play cards that strike for plain,
readable damage, heal you, raise a guard that soaks hits, or draw deeper into
your deck. The Duchess plays back with her own hand: she takes lethal when she
sees it, heals when she is hurt, and never stalls. Reduce her 30 life to zero
before she does the same to you — and watch the table for the turn she turns
the duel around.

Click a card (or press 1–7) to play it, Space to end your turn, R for a
rematch. The whole game is plain HTML, CSS and ES modules with no build step
and no network calls; the screenshots are real renders of its own code.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts   # then open http://localhost:8080
```

Any static file server pointed at `game/` works just as well. Hand the entry
in with `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`.
